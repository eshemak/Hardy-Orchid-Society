Video Competition

Video Competition Rules

  1. Entry is limited to current HOS members, excluding those who make videos professionally.
  2. There is a single class into which each member may enter one video only.
  3. Videos can be on any topic relevant to hardy orchids, wild and/or cultivated.
  4. Videos entered in previous HOS competitions are not permitted.
  5. Videos may last a maximum of 3 minutes.
  6. Videos may include sound but do not have to do so.
  7. Entries must be submitted in a format that can be read by VLC Media Player. This covers most video formats in use in the UK. See VLC Media Player for details.
  8. Videos must have a title that will be seen when projected; credits should not appear on the title page or elsewhere in the video.
  9. Entrants must supply the Organiser with their name, contact details, and the title of their video.
  10. Judging is based on the quality of the videos, not on the rarity of the plants shown.
