About the Society

Committee

The Hardy Orchid Society is supported by a committee that oversees governance, membership administration, events, publications, conservation priorities, and day-to-day coordination.

Get in touch

Committee roles

Typical responsibilities include chairing meetings, keeping records, managing membership enquiries, coordinating publications, and supporting conservation and education activity.

  • Chair and officers provide strategic direction
  • Membership and communications roles support member services
  • Event and conservation roles coordinate specialist programmes
