About the Society

How the society operates

The Society combines volunteer effort, member participation, and a regular programme of communications and events to support study, conservation, and cultivation.

How activity is organised

  • Committee planning supports the annual calendar and member services
  • Meetings and publications share practical and scientific knowledge
  • Regional activity and event reports keep the community connected
  • Conservation and research initiatives are coordinated with partners where appropriate
