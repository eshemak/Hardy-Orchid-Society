Hardy Orchid Society Seed Bank

Members-only availability

Seeds available for members only to purchase.

The Hardy Orchid Society Seed Bank exists to provide members with seed for the artificial propagation of hardy orchids using laboratory and micropropagation techniques.

About the seed bank

In most situations, orchid seed scattered directly into a garden is unlikely to encounter the precise conditions and fungal partners required for successful germination. As a result, much of the seed may be wasted if used in this way.

The seed bank relies on donations from members. If you are able to successfully pollinate your plants and produce fresh seed, please consider donating it to help maintain and expand the collection.

Seed charges

A charge of £3 per sample applies to seed that is less than five years old. A sample typically contains 0.2ml to 0.4ml of seed, which is usually sufficient for two or three laboratory sowings.

Seed that is more than five years old is normally still viable and is provided free of charge while stocks remain available.

Limited quantity seed

Seed marked "Limited Quantity" has usually been collected from only a small number of seed pods and supplies can be exhausted very quickly.

Orders for these species may be restricted to smaller sample sizes, and availability cannot be guaranteed. Members interested in these species are encouraged to order early.

Postage and packing

Postage and packing is charged at:

  • £3.00 via Royal Mail Tracked 48 (recommended)
  • £1.75 via standard untracked post

The postage charge applies regardless of the number of seed samples ordered unless the seed is purchased alongside other consumable items (growing media, fungi, containers & Petri dishes and more).

Please indicate your preferred postal method when ordering.

Seed for meadow creation and garden scattering

Some members may wish to establish orchid-rich areas within their own gardens, plots or managed land. For this purpose, selected seed is available specifically for scattering.

Scattering-eligible species are shown in the dedicated "Seed available for scattering" table on this page. These species are sufficiently plentiful and suitable for distribution outside laboratory conditions.

Currently available are:

  • Epipactis helleborine
  • Dactylorhiza praetermissa var. praetermissa
  • Dactylorhiza praetermissa var. junialis
  • Dactylorhiza fuchsii

This seed is supplied in 5ml paper-envelope quantities and is free of charge to members whilst stocks last. Standard postage charges apply.

Because this material is not cleaned to laboratory standards, it is intended for scattering and not for artificial propagation.

Mixed meadow seed

A mixed orchid seed collection intended specifically for scattering is available separately from HOS member Dave Trudgill, who has successfully used this approach to enrich his own orchid meadow in Scotland.

For further information, please contact: davetrudgill@googlemail.com

Any proceeds from these sales are donated to the Society.

Important notice

Seed obtained from the Hardy Orchid Society Seed Bank must not be released into wild environments.

The Society expects all members to use supplied seed responsibly and only within cultivated or managed settings.

Additional enquiries

Members interested in propagating rarer species or unusual hybrids may wish to contact Seed Bank Manager John Haggar.

Small quantities of legally obtained seed from additional species may occasionally be available. These accessions are often quite old and viability cannot be guaranteed.
