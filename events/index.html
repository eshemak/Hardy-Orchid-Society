National meetings programme

The HOS holds three one-day meetings during the year, spread around the country. The Spring Meeting, which includes the brief Annual General Meeting and Plant Show, is normally held in the Oxford area. The Autumn Northern Meeting is usually held in September in northern England. The Autumn Southern Meeting, the final meeting of the year, is held near the end of October in southern England and includes the annual Photographic Show.

Each meeting includes talks and slide shows covering all aspects of hardy orchids. If you would like to give a presentation at one of these meetings, the Chairman will be pleased to hear from you. A useful way to begin as a first-time speaker is through the “5 Slides in 5 Minutes” sessions.

Meetings usually include commercial plant sales tables and a Members’ Table, where members may sell surplus plants and orchid-related sundries; 10% of receipts go to HOS. There is also lunch and dedicated time for members to discuss growing challenges and successes. Full meeting details and booking forms are circulated in the HOS Journal and published on this website.
