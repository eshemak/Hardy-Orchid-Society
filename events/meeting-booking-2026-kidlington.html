Hardy Orchid Society Autumn Meeting and Photographic Competition 2026

Holding page for the online booking form. Meeting date: Sunday 15 November 2026, Exeter Hall, Oxford Rd, Kidlington, Oxon, OX5 1AB.

Booking and payment details

A fee is payable to cover meeting costs, including tea and coffee. Lunch is optional. Non-members are welcome if accompanying a member and paying the appropriate fee (with or without lunch).

Sales tables must be booked in advance at £25.00. Members using the Members’ Sales Table are responsible for their own sales and receipts; 10% of proceeds go to HOS.

  • Meeting fee: £12.00 member / £15.00 guest
  • Optional lunch: £11.00
  • Sales table: £25.00

Electronic payment option: send a scanned or electronically completed booking form to tarrant.simon@outlook.com and pay by bank transfer to Hardy Orchid Society, HSBC UK Bank Plc, sort code 40-46-06, account number 21185527. Include payment reference: Surname-Kidl-HOS membership number.

Postal option: send the form and cheque (including membership number and fee). Cheques payable to Hardy Orchid Society.

Postal address (arrive before 31 October 2026): Simon Tarrant, Bumbys, Fox Road, Mashbury, Chelmsford, CM1 4TJ.

Queries: tarrant.simon@outlook.com

Information requested on the booking form

  • Name(s), clearly printed
  • Membership number (or Guest)

  • Total charge per person and total people charge
  • Members’ Sales Table request (Yes/No)
