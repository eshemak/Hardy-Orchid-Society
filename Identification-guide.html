Identification guide

British & Irish Orchids

A photographic guide to the wild orchids of Britain and Ireland, prepared as an aid to identification. Each species profile includes images of habitats, whole plants and close-ups, together with descriptive notes.

The species are arranged in taxonomic groups below. Select a name to open its profile page; photographs and identification information will be added over time.
