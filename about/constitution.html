About the Society

Constitution

The Society’s constitution sets out its purpose, governance arrangements, and the expectations that support fair and effective operation.

What the constitution covers

  • The charitable or educational purpose of the Society
  • How membership is managed and renewed
  • Committee responsibilities and decision-making
  • Meetings, annual reporting, and accountability

Members can use this framework to understand how the Society is run and how formal decisions are made.
