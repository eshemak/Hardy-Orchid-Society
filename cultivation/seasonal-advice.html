Growing Hardy Orchids

Seasonal advice

Seasonal timing matters: hardy orchids change visibly through the year, and care should follow those natural rhythms.

Season-by-season thinking

  • Spring: monitor emergence, growth, and flowering
  • Summer: maintain suitable moisture and observe active growth
  • Autumn: prepare for dormancy or winter-green growth depending on the plant
  • Winter: protect, observe, and avoid unnecessary disturbance
