Growing Hardy Orchids

Pests & Diseases

Early observation and good hygiene are the best tools for keeping orchid collections healthy and avoiding avoidable losses.

Common focus areas

  • Monitoring for damage, rot, or distorted growth
  • Improving airflow, hygiene, and drainage
  • Separating or reviewing affected plants promptly
  • Adjusting watering and cultural conditions before problems escalate
