HOS Conservation Grant Awards

Recent awards supported practical habitat restoration, propagation and long-term management work for wild orchids.

Monkton Nature Reserve, Kent

Monkton Nature Reserve in Kent is using the grant to contribute orchid-specific conservation to a larger project to reduce scrub in the lower areas of the reserve, increasing chalk grassland by 10%.

After several reserve staff attended the annual HOS Seed Propagation workshop last year, the reserve set up a propagation area to increase numbers of the many orchid species on site.

The Warden has also worked on controlling rabbit predation. Cages are used for rare species such as Lizard Orchid, and an odour spray applied to vulnerable plants has helped populations of several species, including Man Orchid, to expand.

Herefordshire Meadows

Herefordshire Meadows, a charity supporting flower-rich grasslands, has researched the status of Green-winged Orchid and found that around a third of 27 known sites are not viable, often containing only one plant.

Using detailed soil and site analysis, the charity identified donor sites where Anacamptis morio is thriving and ten matching receptor sites where seed will be spread and monitored.

This work is being supported by a tailored management plan for landowners who have received seed.
