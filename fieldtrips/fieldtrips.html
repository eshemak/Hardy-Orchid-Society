Booking a trip with HOS

Thank you to all leaders who volunteer their time. Many leaders and members have deep orchid-site experience, and many also bring knowledge of birds, butterflies, dragonflies and wider natural history.

You must decide whether you are fit and healthy enough for each walk. Difficulty can vary with weather and underfoot conditions, and many sites are uneven and remote. Participants are responsible for their own safety. Leaders do not carry first-aid or emergency equipment, and some areas have no mobile signal.

We usually collect a voluntary donation for non-government organisations (for example Wildlife Trusts) that maintain visited sites. HOS insurance covers members only, so bringing non-member guests is usually not possible. Dogs are not permitted unless the leader explicitly agrees. Trips may be rescheduled or cancelled if flowering times are affected by seasonal conditions.

Register for any trip by emailing the trip leader. Leader email addresses are published in the Spring 2026 issue of the Journal of the Hardy Orchid Society. Include your HOS membership number. General field-trip enquiries: fieldtrips@hardyorchidsociety.org.

Please remember: you must be a member of the Hardy Orchid Society to book a field trip.

Field Trips in 2026

Tuesday 26 May: East Kent

Leader: Colin Sillence

Parkgate and Denge Wood (“Bonsai Bank”) for Monkey, Lady, Fly and Greater Butterfly orchids; up to twelve orchid species possible. Less than two miles of easy walking. Car sharing required due to limited parking.

This field trip is now fully booked.

Thursday 28 May: Warburg Reserve, Henley-on-Thames

Leader: Keith Boseley

BBOWT reserve in the Chilterns with Fly, Bird’s Nest, Early Purple and Greater Butterfly orchids expected, plus other species and helleborines in leaf. Includes steep inclines.

This field trip is now fully booked.

Saturday 30 May: Box Hill, Surrey

Leader: Richard Kulczycki

Box Hill to Brockham Quarry, around ten miles with some steep sections (strenuous). Around ten orchid species expected. Option to join mid-afternoon for Brockham Quarry.

Thursday 4 June: Folkestone and East Kent sites

Leader: Ian Hadingham

Sites for Ophrys fuciflora, Orchis purpurea (including var. alba), potential Ophrys insectifera, Platanthera bifolia, Neottia nidus-avis, then Monkton Nature Reserve and Sandwich/Sandwich Bay for Himantoglossum hircinum. Significant driving involved.

This field trip is now fully booked.

Saturday 13 June: Braunton Burrows, North Devon

Leader: Roger Harding

Three-hour walk, not strenuous but weather-exposed; mostly firm ground with some damp slacks. Guided by one of John and Mary Breeds. Orchid species may include Early and Southern Marsh-orchid (incl. hybrids and coccinea), Bee, Pyramidal and Twayblade.

Sunday 14 June: Waitby Greenriggs, Cumbria

Leader: Charlie Philpotts

One of Britain’s best orchid reserves with multiple species through the season. Fly Orchids expected to be at their best in early June, with additional orchids and wildflowers.

Saturday 20 June: Three sites near Leeds

Leader: Charlie Philpotts

Former mining site, limestone outcrop and old limestone meadow. Expected species include Northern and Southern Marsh-orchid, Common Spotted-orchid and hybrids, Twayblades and Bee Orchids.

Saturday 20 June: Windsor Hill, near Princes Risborough

Registrations: fieldtrips@hardyorchidsociety.org

Trip to see Red Helleborine. Approx. one mile total walking. Flowering cannot be guaranteed and date may move. Donations collected to cover BBOWT guide fees and conservation support.

Sunday 21 June: Kenfig Dunes, Glamorgan

Leader: Steve Parsons

Primary focus on Fen orchid, with likely high-summer species including Early Marsh-orchid var. coccinea. Fair amount of walking through extensive dunes.

This field trip is now fully booked.

Saturday 27 June: Ladle Hill, Hampshire Downs

Leader: Simon Melville

Late-flowering Burnt Orchids, Frog Orchids, Pyramidal Orchids (possibly f. emarginata), Common Spotted-orchids, Chalk Fragrant-orchids and more. Easy 25-minute walk from parking, with uneven ground.

This field trip is now fully booked.

Saturday 4 July: Noar Hill, North Hampshire

Leader: Ken & Gillian Elsom

Site managed by Hampshire & Isle of Wight Wildlife Trust. Expect Musk, Common Spotted, Pyramidal and Chalk Fragrant-orchid, plus Fly Orchids, Twayblades and possible Violet Helleborines. Some steep paths that can be slippery.

Fully booked – waiting list only.

Sunday 12 July: Cumbria

Leader: Charlie Philpotts

Waitby Greenriggs and Augill Pasture, meeting in Kirkby Stephen. Expected orchids include Northern Marsh-orchid, Common Spotted-orchid, Heath Spotted-orchid, Greater and Lesser Butterfly-orchid, Marsh Helleborine and three Fragrant-orchid varieties, plus hybrids.

Saturday 18 July: Braunton Burrows, North Devon

Leader: Roger Harding

Second trip with a different route and later-season orchids. Three-hour walk, mostly firm and dry with some damp slacks. Guided by Mary Breeds. Expected species include Marsh Helleborine in large numbers, Marsh Fragrant-orchid, Pyramidal Orchid and Twayblade.
